REMOTE ONE

Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

1. Overview

Remote One is a smart TV remote control application provided by EEB Consultancy LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Remote One.

Remote One is designed primarily to communicate directly with compatible televisions and devices on your local network. We aim to collect as little personal information as reasonably necessary to provide the app.

2. Local Network Access

Remote One requires access to your local network to discover, connect to, pair with, and control compatible televisions and devices on the same network as your iPhone.

During this process, Remote One may process technical information made available by devices on your local network, such as device names, manufacturer or platform information, model information, network addresses, connection status, capabilities, and other information necessary to establish and maintain a connection.

This information is used to provide the remote-control functionality of the app and is not used to track you across apps or websites.

3. TV Pairing and Connection Information

Some compatible televisions require pairing, authorization, or connection credentials before they can be controlled.

Remote One may store information required to reconnect to previously paired devices, including device identifiers and authorization credentials provided by the television or device.

Where appropriate, sensitive connection credentials are stored using secure storage mechanisms available on your device. These credentials are used only to communicate with the associated television or device.

4. Saved TVs and App Preferences

Remote One may store information on your device about televisions you have connected to, along with app preferences and settings required to provide features such as saved TVs, quick switching, connection preferences, and remote-control behavior.

This information is used to operate the app and provide a consistent experience between sessions.

5. Keyboard and Remote Commands

Remote One may allow you to send text, navigation commands, media commands, and other supported remote-control actions to a connected television.

Text entered for transmission to your television is used to perform the requested action. Remote One does not use the text you send to your television for advertising or cross-app tracking.

6. Purchases and Subscriptions

Remote One offers optional paid features through in-app purchases and subscriptions processed by Apple through the App Store.

EEB Consultancy LLC does not directly receive or store your full payment card information. Apple processes payments and provides the app with transaction and entitlement information necessary to determine whether you have access to Remote One Premium.

Apple's handling of payment and account information is governed by Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

7. Information We Do Not Use for Advertising

Remote One does not use information obtained through local TV discovery, pairing, remote commands, or text input to build advertising profiles or track you across apps and websites owned by other companies.

8. Third-Party Devices and Services

Remote One communicates with compatible televisions, streaming devices, operating systems, and related services provided by third parties.

When you interact with a third-party device or service, that third party may process information according to its own privacy policy and terms. EEB Consultancy LLC is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party manufacturers, platforms, or services.

9. Data Retention

Information stored locally by Remote One is retained as needed to provide features such as saved device connections and app preferences. You may remove saved televisions or delete the app to remove locally stored app data, subject to information retained independently by Apple or third-party devices and services.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures designed to protect information handled by Remote One. However, no electronic storage or network communication method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

The security and availability of communications with your television also depend on the protocols and security features provided by the television manufacturer or platform.

11. Children's Privacy

Remote One is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Remote One, applicable requirements, or our practices. When we make changes, we will update the effective date shown at the top of this page.
